Food Coma: Why Do I Feel So Sleepy?

You’ve had that big meal and now all you want is a nap. Here’s why.

The Real Reason

Blood flow shift: After eating, blood rushes to your gut to help digest food. That means less blood goes to your brain, so you feel tired.

Insulin spike: Carbs turn into sugar fast. Insulin rises to handle it, which helps tryptophan get into your brain. That makes serotonin and melatonin - the sleepy chemicals.

Worst offenders: Pasta, rice, pizza, soda. Big meals that are high in carbs + fat hit the hardest.

How to Avoid It

  • Eat smaller portions
  • Add protein and fiber to balance carbs
  • Take a 10 min walk after eating
